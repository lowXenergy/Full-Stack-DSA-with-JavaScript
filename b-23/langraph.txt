LangGraph => Used to make work flow. 
Ai can't work with long work which consider long steps eg writting an article (REsearch , OUtline article , create article, check copy right issues(if copyright found then rewrite the article)).Ai may or may not follow all these instructions. To make ai follow these instruction we use LangGraph.
Inshort when we want ai to perform some set of instruction that too in sequence we use LangGraph.
LangGraph:
    node-[llm,action]
    edges-[decide the sequence of the workflow i.e direction of the flow]
